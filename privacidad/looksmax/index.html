Privacy Policy

Looksmax Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Looksmax mobile application (the "Application"), provided by Lanchas Dev (the "Service Provider"). Contact: lanchasdev@gmail.com

The short version: the Application collects nothing. It has no accounts, no login and no registration, it shows no advertising, it contains no analytics, and it does not send anything anywhere. It has no server behind it, because it does not need one.

1. What the Application stores, and where

Everything you do in the Application is written to your device's own storage and stays there:

  • Your habits, routines, missions, contracts, skills and packs, and whatever progress, streaks and results they produce.
  • The results of the habit wheel.
  • Your chosen language and whether you have finished the introduction.

None of it is transmitted. There is no account to attach it to, no backup in the cloud and no copy held by us. If you change device, the data does not travel with you — that is the cost of a design with no server, and we would rather state it than quietly upload your data to solve it.

2. Network activity

The Application makes no network requests of its own. It works with the device in flight mode, and it behaves identically online and offline. The spinning wheel is a small web page bundled inside the Application itself, not a page loaded from the internet.

There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting and no remote configuration. Nothing about your device, your usage or your habits reaches us or any third party.

3. Permissions

The Application asks for one optional permission: notifications, so it can remind you about a habit at the time you chose. Those reminders are scheduled on the device. The Application does not obtain a push token, there is no notification server, and we cannot send you anything. Declining the permission only means no reminders; everything else works the same.

The Application does not ask for the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts, your photos or your health data, and does not read your advertising identifier.

4. Third parties

The Application integrates no third-party service. Apple and Google know that you downloaded it, in the same way they know about every app you install, and that happens on the store's side under Apple's and Google's own privacy policies. We receive nothing from them beyond aggregate download and crash statistics that identify nobody.

We do not sell data. We have none to sell.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • We retain nothing, because we receive nothing.
  • Delete everything on Android: Settings → Apps → Looksmax → Storage → Clear data.
  • Delete everything on iOS: delete the Application from the Home Screen or from Settings → General → iPhone Storage.
  • Support emails. If you write to us, your message and address are kept for at most 6 months, unless a legal obligation requires longer. That is the only personal data we ever hold about anyone, and it exists only because you chose to write.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

In practice there is nothing here to exercise them against: we hold no data about you, so there is no record for us to show you, correct or erase. Everything the Application knows is on your device, and deleting the Application deletes it. We say this plainly rather than reciting rights we have no data to honour.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the Application collects nothing, there is nothing about a child to collect. If a parent or guardian still has a concern, write to lanchasdev@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy — most likely because the Application starts doing something new. Changes take effect when the revised version is posted, with a new "Last updated" date. If a future version ever begins collecting data, this page will say so before that version reaches you.
